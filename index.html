Case Study

How I built T4 Tea For U

From zero online presence to a full ordering system

T4 Tea For U is a boba tea shop in Folsom, CA. They had no website, no online ordering, and were losing delivery orders to competitors with better digital presence. They needed a site that matched the quality of their drinks — premium, inviting, and functional.

I built a dark cinematic website with a 92-item online ordering menu, integrated delivery platform links (DoorDash, UberEats, GrubHub), real product photography, and a fully responsive design — all hand-coded in a single HTML file with zero dependencies.
