Case Study · Food & Beverage

Turning a Folsom boba shop into a cinematic brand online

T4 Tea For U had a 99/100 health-inspection score and a loyal local following. Their website didn't communicate either. We rebuilt it to match the premium, community-first feeling the shop already had in person.

Client T4 Tea For U · Folsom
Industry Boba / Bubble Tea
Scope Design & Dev · 1 week
Status t4-folsom.vercel.app

01The Ask

A woman-owned boba shop in Broadstone Plaza needed a website that matched the way the shop actually feels when you walk in: vibrant, clean, community-rooted. The existing template-style site read as generic — same stock photos, same layout as every other boba brand.

The real problem wasn't missing pages. It was missing personality. Prospective customers searching "boba Folsom" needed to see what makes T4 different — healthier ingredients, free boba with every drink, a real location worth visiting — in the first three seconds on the site.

02The Approach

Four operator-level decisions shaped the build:

01

Lead with location, not menu

Locals search by neighborhood. The hero answers "where" before "what," pinning the Broadstone Plaza address and hours above the fold — so someone on their phone at a red light gets the answer in one tap.

02

Brand colors over stock photos

We built the palette from T4's existing logo — teal, gold, and cream — so the site feels continuous with the in-store experience. No generic boba-shop stock imagery. Every visual decision reinforces "this is T4, not just another boba place."

03

Menu as storytelling, not spreadsheet

Most boba sites dump a PDF menu. We grouped drinks into categories with short descriptions so first-time customers know where to start. The menu doubles as onboarding for people who've never ordered boba.

04

Performance over polish

Mobile load speed matters more than desktop aesthetic for this audience. We kept bundle size lean, skipped heavy libraries, and made sure the site loads under 2 seconds on a throttled 4G connection.

03The Build

Shipped in a single focused build week:

→

Hero with location & hours, not a hero image

First thing visitors see: address, hours, phone. The decision a local customer actually needs to make.

→

Menu gallery organized by drink type

Milk tea, fruit tea, slush, and specialty categories — each with their own visual identity — so regulars and first-timers both orient fast.

→

Mobile-first, zero compromise

Over 70% of boba searches happen on phones. Every interaction was designed touch-first: oversized tap targets, no hover-only menus, scroll-friendly layout.

→

Order CTA that actually converts

Clear, repeating "Order Now" anchors throughout the site — not buried in a nav dropdown. Friction-free path from "I want boba" to "I'm ordering."

"Build sites for the customer's attention span, not the designer's portfolio." — the principle running through every decision on this project.

05The Result

Shipped and live at t4-folsom.vercel.app. The site reflects the shop's actual brand instead of a template version of it. Menu is navigable in seconds, location is unmissable, and the brand reads consistent with the in-store experience.
